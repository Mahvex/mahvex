# Mahvex Community Guidelines

**Last Updated: September 2026**

Mahvex aims to provide a respectful, useful, and inclusive
environment for developers, programmers, learners, and technology
enthusiasts.

These Community Guidelines describe the behavior expected from
everyone participating in Mahvex communities and services.

## 1. Be Respectful

Treat other members with respect.

Harassment, personal attacks, threats, intimidation, or targeted
abuse are not acceptable.

Disagreements are normal, but discussions should remain
constructive and focused on the subject.

## 2. Keep Discussions Constructive

When participating in discussions, issues, comments, or other
community spaces:

- Share useful and relevant information.
- Explain your ideas clearly.
- Respect different technical opinions.
- Avoid unnecessary arguments.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, products, or technical decisions,
  not on individuals.

## 3. No Harassment or Discrimination

Mahvex does not tolerate harassment or discriminatory behavior
directed at individuals or groups.

This includes abusive behavior based on characteristics such as
race, nationality, ethnicity, gender, religion, disability, age,
or other protected characteristics where applicable.

## 4. No Malicious or Harmful Activity

Community spaces must not be used to:

- Distribute malware or malicious code.
- Attempt unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
- Facilitate cyber abuse or attacks.
- Distribute stolen credentials or private information.
- Promote fraudulent activity.
- Intentionally disrupt Mahvex services or other users.

## 5. Protect Privacy

Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information
without appropriate authorization.

This includes passwords, authentication credentials, private
communications, personal contact information, and other sensitive
information.

## 6. Keep Content Relevant

Content posted in Mahvex community spaces should be reasonably
related to the purpose of the relevant discussion, service, or
community.

Spam, excessive self-promotion, misleading content, and repeated
irrelevant posts may be removed.

## 7. Intellectual Property

Do not submit or distribute content that you do not have the right
to use.

Respect applicable copyrights, trademarks, licenses, and other
intellectual property rights.

## 8. Security Reports

Security vulnerabilities should not be publicly disclosed in a
way that could unnecessarily put users or systems at risk.

Security issues should be reported through the security contact
or process provided by Mahvex.

See:

[Security Policy](SECURITY.md)

## 9. Enforcement

Mahvex may take reasonable action when these guidelines are
violated.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- Removing content.
- Issuing a warning.
- Restricting participation.
- Suspending access to a service.
- Terminating access where necessary.

Actions may depend on the severity and circumstances of the
violation.

## 10. Good-Faith Participation

Mahvex encourages users to participate in good faith and help
create an environment where developers can learn, build, share,
and collaborate.

## 11. Reporting Problems

If you encounter behavior that violates these guidelines, you can
contact the Mahvex team:

**Email:** mahvex.official@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for the team to understand and
review the issue.

## 12. Changes to These Guidelines

Mahvex may update these guidelines as the platform and its
communities evolve.

The latest version published in this repository represents the
current version.
