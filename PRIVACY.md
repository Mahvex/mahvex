# Mahvex Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 2026**

Mahvex respects user privacy and is committed to protecting
personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains, at a general level, how Mahvex
may handle information when users access or interact with the
Mahvex platform.

## 1. About This Policy

Mahvex is a technology platform designed for developers and
programmers.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mahvex website, platform,
and services that explicitly reference this policy.

Individual third-party services integrated with or linked from
Mahvex may have their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with Mahvex, the platform may
receive information such as:

- Information you voluntarily provide to us.
- Contact information when you contact Mahvex.
- Technical information required to operate and secure the
  platform.
- Basic information about interactions with Mahvex services.
- Information provided through specific features that require
  user input.

Mahvex does not intentionally request personal information that
is not necessary for the operation of a particular service.

## 3. Contact Information

If you contact Mahvex through email or another available
communication channel, we may receive the information contained
in your message.

This information may be used to:

- Respond to your request.
- Provide support.
- Investigate reported issues.
- Improve Mahvex services.

## 4. Technical and Security Information

Like most online services, Mahvex may process limited technical
information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure its
infrastructure.

This may include information such as:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Request information
- Security and diagnostic information

The exact information collected may vary depending on the
services and infrastructure being used.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Operate and maintain Mahvex.
- Improve the quality and reliability of services.
- Respond to users and support requests.
- Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
- Analyze technical problems.
- Develop and improve new features and services.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 6. Data Sharing

Mahvex does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to:

- Provide a requested service.
- Operate infrastructure or technical services.
- Protect the security of Mahvex and its users.
- Comply with applicable law or legal requirements.

Third-party providers used by Mahvex may process information
according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Mahvex may link to or integrate with third-party services.

These services may collect or process information independently
from Mahvex.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services
before providing personal information to them.

## 8. Data Security

Mahvex takes reasonable technical and organizational measures
to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration,
disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be
completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention

Mahvex retains information only for as long as reasonably
necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to
maintain services, resolve issues, meet security requirements,
or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose
of the information.

## 10. User Rights

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information,
users may have rights concerning their personal information,
including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or
other forms of data control.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Mahvex is designed primarily for developers, programmers,
students, and technology users.

Mahvex does not knowingly collect personal information from
children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

## 12. Beta / Experimental Status

Mahvex is currently in a Beta / Experimental phase.

Privacy-related practices may evolve as new features,
integrations, and services are introduced.

When a material change affects this Privacy Policy, the updated
version will be published with a new "Last Updated" date.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

Mahvex may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The latest version published in this repository represents the
current version of this policy.
